PDF Editor: frequently asked questions

Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?

PDFs store text as fixed shapes, so no browser editor can retype it perfectly. The reliable method is to cover the old text with the Whiteout tool and type the new text on top with the Text tool. The result looks like an edit in any PDF reader.

How do I sign a PDF?

Click Sign, draw your signature with your mouse or finger, then click on the page where it should go. You can move and resize it before saving.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. The editor runs entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never sent to a server, which makes it safe for contracts, forms and personal documents.

Is the PDF editor really free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of files or pages.

Can I delete pages?

Yes. Each page has a Delete page button. Deleted pages are greyed out and left out of the saved file; click Restore page to bring one back.

Which characters can I type?

The built-in PDF fonts support English and most Western European letters and symbols. Characters outside that set, such as Urdu, Arabic or Chinese, are replaced with a question mark.
