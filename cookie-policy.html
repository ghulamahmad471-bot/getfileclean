Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on getfileclean.com. Read it together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and device identifiers. They can remember settings, measure visits or help show relevant ads.

Cookies we set

The GetFileClean Tool does not need cookies to work, and we do not currently set our own cookies. Your files are processed in your browser and are not stored in cookies.

Third-party cookies

Advertising (Google AdSense). If ads are shown on the Website, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads, limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance and, with your consent where required, personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Learn more at How Google uses cookies in advertising.

Analytics. If we use analytics, analytics cookies may count visits and show which pages are used, in aggregate.

Fonts. Google Fonts may be loaded from Google's servers. This doesn't set cookies for our pages, but Google receives your IP address to deliver the fonts.

Consent

Where the law requires it, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message will ask you to accept or reject non-essential cookies before they are used. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy or cookie settings link shown on the Website.

How to manage cookies

  • Personalised ads: visit Google Ads Settings, aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com.
  • Your browser: most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking cookies won't stop the GetFileClean Tool from working.
