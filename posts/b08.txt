Online sellers take and upload hundreds of product photos. Each one may carry **metadata** that has nothing to do with the product.

## What product photos can reveal

- **Where your stock is kept:** GPS coordinates of your home, warehouse or studio.
- **When you work:** timestamps on every photo.

- **Security:** stock locations are useful information for thieves.
- **Competitive privacy:** competitors can learn about your setup or suppliers.
- **Page speed:** metadata adds a little to every image's file size. Across a large catalogue, cleaner files mean slightly faster pages.
- **Professionalism:** clean images look and behave consistently.

## Marketplace and store platforms

Some marketplaces process images, but you also send photos to customers, wholesale buyers, ad platforms and designers. Cleaning at the source covers every channel.

=== does-image-metadata-affect-seo
title: Does Image Metadata Affect SEO? EXIF, IPTC and Rankings
cat: Ecommerce & Business
kw: image metadata seo, does exif affect seo, image seo metadata, iptc google images, remove exif seo
desc: Does image metadata affect SEO? What Google has said about EXIF, the IPTC fields it can show, and whether removing photo metadata hurts your rankings.
---
Website owners sometimes worry that removing photo metadata will hurt their search rankings. Here's what we know about **image metadata and SEO**.

## EXIF data and rankings

Google representatives have said publicly that Google doesn't rely on EXIF data such as camera model or GPS for ranking images in search. Removing EXIF is very unlikely to affect your rankings.

## IPTC fields Google can display

Google Images can show certain IPTC photo metadata, such as creator, credit line and copyright notice, and licensing details. If you rely on those credits appearing, keep those fields, which means not stripping all metadata from those particular images.

## What actually matters for image SEO

- **Descriptive file names**, such as "red-leather-wallet.jpg"
- **Alt text** describing the image
- **Relevant surrounding text** on the page
- **Fast loading:** compressed images in modern formats, sized correctly
- **Structured data** for products where relevant
- **Image sitemaps** for large sites

## Metadata and page speed

Metadata adds bytes to every image. On its own it's usually small, but some photos carry large blocks of data or embedded thumbnails. Removing it trims file size, which supports faster pages.

## The bottom line

For most sites, removing EXIF and other personal metadata has no downside for SEO and a small benefit for speed and privacy. Keep IPTC credit fields only on images where you want credits shown.

=== company-data-leaks-metadata
title: How Metadata Causes Company Data Leaks (and How to Prevent Them)
cat: Ecommerce & Business
kw: metadata data leak, company metadata leak, document metadata breach, prevent metadata leaks, corporate document security
desc: Metadata has caused embarrassing and costly leaks of internal information. Learn the common patterns behind metadata leaks and how companies prevent them.
---
Some of the most avoidable information leaks happen through **metadata** and hidden document content rather than hacking.

## Common leak patterns

**1. Tracked changes in published documents.** Reports, contracts and statements published with revision history intact, showing earlier wording.

**2. Author and company properties.** Documents claiming to be independent but carrying an author or company name pointing elsewhere.

**3. Fake redaction.** PDFs where sensitive text was covered with black boxes but not actually removed.

**4. Photo location.** Images posted online that reveal sensitive sites through GPS data.

**5. Reused templates.** Proposals or letters with properties naming another client.

These patterns have appeared in public cases involving governments, law firms and companies. They're simple mistakes with outsized consequences.

## Prevention

- **Policy:** decide when files must be cleaned, for example anything going outside the company.
- **Training:** a short session on metadata, track changes and proper redaction.
- **Tools:** give staff an easy tool that works without uploading files to third parties.
- **Checks:** include metadata in review steps for public documents.

=== metadata-and-gdpr
title: Metadata and GDPR: Is Metadata Personal Data?
cat: Privacy & Security
kw: metadata gdpr, is metadata personal data, gdpr file metadata, data minimisation metadata, privacy law metadata
desc: Can file metadata be personal data under GDPR? How author names, GPS and device data fit into data protection, and practical steps to minimise metadata.
---
Under privacy laws like the EU's GDPR, personal data means information relating to an identified or identifiable person. Much **file metadata** fits that description.

## Metadata that can be personal data

- **Author and editor names** in documents
- **GPS coordinates** in photos taken at someone's home
- **Device serial numbers** that can be linked to a person
- **Timestamps** combined with other details
- **Names in comments** and tracked changes

## Data minimisation

GDPR includes the principle of data minimisation: personal data should be adequate, relevant and limited to what's necessary. Sending documents full of names and location data that the recipient doesn't need sits awkwardly with that principle.

## Practical steps

1. Remove metadata from files shared outside the organisation where it isn't needed.
2. Clean images before publishing them on websites.
3. Include metadata in your privacy training.
4. Prefer tools that don't upload files to third parties, since uploading personal data to another service can create further obligations.

## When you must keep metadata

Some legal, regulatory and records-management duties require preserving original files with metadata. Keep originals where required and clean only the copies you share.

## Disclaimer

This article is general information, not legal advice. Talk to your data protection officer or a lawyer about your situation.

GetFileClean processes files entirely in the browser, so no personal data is transferred to us.

=== metadata-policy-for-teams
title: How to Create a Metadata Policy for Your Team
cat: Ecommerce & Business
kw: metadata policy, document metadata policy, team file sharing policy, metadata removal process, company metadata guidelines
desc: A simple template for a team metadata policy: when to remove metadata, which files to check, who's responsible, and tools that make it easy.
---
A **metadata policy** doesn't need to be long. It needs to be clear enough that people follow it. Here's a simple structure.

## 1. Purpose

One or two sentences: "To prevent personal and confidential information hidden in files from being shared unintentionally."

## 2. When files must be cleaned

- Files sent to anyone outside the organisation
- Files published on websites or social media
- Files submitted to tenders, courts or regulators, unless rules require original metadata
- Photos taken on location

## 3. What to check

- Document properties: author, company, title, template
- Tracked changes, comments and hidden text
- Photo location and device data
- PDF properties and proper redaction

## 4. Exceptions

- Records that must be preserved with original metadata
- Digitally signed documents
- Files where copyright or credit metadata is required

## 5. Tools

Name the approved tools. For example: Word's Document Inspector for comments and tracked changes, and GetFileClean for removing metadata from all file types without uploading them.

## 6. Responsibility

The person sending or publishing a file is responsible for cleaning it. Managers check high-risk documents.

## 7. Training

Include a ten-minute walkthrough in onboarding. Show one real example of metadata in a file. It's more convincing than any rule.

## Keep it practical

The best policy is one people actually follow. A fast, free, no-install tool removes the main reason people skip the step.
