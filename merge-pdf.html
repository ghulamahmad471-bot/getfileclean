Why merge PDFs with GetFileClean?

Most online PDF mergers upload your documents to their servers, process them there and send them back. That's a privacy risk for contracts, invoices, ID scans and anything else you wouldn't email to a stranger. GetFileClean works differently: the merging code runs inside your web browser, so your files never leave your computer or phone. You can even disconnect from the internet once the page has loaded.

There are no daily limits, no file size caps and no watermark on the result. By default the merged PDF also has its hidden metadata removed, such as the author name, the software used and creation dates, so you can share it without giving away extra details. Merge PDF: frequently asked questions

Is it safe to merge PDF files here?

Yes. GetFileClean merges your PDFs inside your own browser. The files are never uploaded to a server, so nobody else can see them.

Is there a limit on file size or the number of files?

There are no fixed limits. You can combine as many PDFs as your device's memory can handle. Very large files (hundreds of MB) may be slow on older phones.

Can I change the order of the files?

Yes. Drag files up or down in the list, or use the arrow buttons, before you click Merge PDF. The files are combined from top to bottom.

Can I add images to the merged PDF?

Yes. You can add JPG and PNG images to the list. Each image becomes one page, sized to fit the image.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No. Encrypted PDFs need to be unlocked with their password first. Remove the password, then add the file again.

Does the merged PDF keep my metadata?

By default, the merged file has its hidden metadata (author, title, software, dates) removed. You can untick the option if you want to keep it.
