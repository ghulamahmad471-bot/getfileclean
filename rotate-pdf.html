Fix sideways and upside-down PDF pages

Scanned documents and phone photos turned into PDFs often end up sideways or upside down. Many viewers let you rotate a page on screen, but the change isn't saved, so the next person who opens the file sees it the wrong way again. GetFileClean writes the rotation into the PDF itself, so the pages stay correct in every app.

Like all our tools, Rotate PDF runs in your browser. Your document isn't uploaded anywhere, there's no file size limit and no watermark. You can also remove hidden details such as the author and editing software when you save. To clean metadata from other files too, use our free metadata remover.

Rotate PDF: frequently asked questions

Will the rotation be saved permanently?

Yes. The new orientation is written into the PDF file itself, so the pages stay rotated when you open the file in any PDF reader, browser or app.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Each page has its own rotate buttons. You can turn single pages, or use Rotate all to turn every page at once.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is opened and rotated in your browser on your own device. It is never sent to a server.

Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page's orientation setting. Text, images and links stay exactly as they were.

Can I rotate a password-protected PDF?

No. Remove the password first, then open the file here again.
