Questions

Is it really free with no limits?

Yes. Your own device does the work, so there is no server cost and no cap on file size or number of files. Very large videos are limited only by your device's memory.

Will cleaning change how my file looks or plays?

No. JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images are not re-compressed. Documents keep all their content and formatting. Videos and audio keep the same picture and sound. Only HEIC, AVIF, BMP and TIFF images are converted to JPG or PNG.

Why are photos sometimes turned?

Phones often save photos sideways with a tag that tells viewers to rotate them. That tag is metadata, so GetFileClean rotates the actual picture upright before removing it.

What about digitally signed or password-protected PDFs?

A digital signature stops being valid once anything in a signed PDF changes, so you will see a warning on signed files. Password-protected PDFs cannot be opened and are skipped.

Does it remove comments and tracked changes in Word files?

No. Those are part of the document's content. Use Word's built-in Inspect Document tool to remove them, then clean the file here.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any recent version of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
