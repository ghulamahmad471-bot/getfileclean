Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GetFileClean ("we", "us", "our"), operated by AR ECOM SERVICE LLC, handles information when you use getfileclean.com (the "Website") and the metadata removal tool on it (the "Tool").

Summary

  • Your files never leave your device. The Tool processes files inside your web browser. We do not receive, upload, store or see the files you clean.
  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name or email to use the Tool.
  • Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical logs.
  • The Website may show ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies. You can control personalised advertising (details below).

1. Files you process with the Tool

When you add a file to the Tool, it is read and cleaned by code running in your own browser. The file and the cleaned copy stay on your device. They are not transmitted to our servers or to any third party. When you close or reload the page, the files are removed from the browser's memory.

2. Information collected automatically

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider (Hostinger) automatically records standard server log information, which may include your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit, the referring page and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Website and is kept only as long as needed for those purposes.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not currently set our own cookies to run the Tool. Google Analytics, and other third parties including Google advertising, may place and read cookies or use similar technologies (such as web beacons and device identifiers) on your browser when you visit the Website, as described below. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the Website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Website and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.com.
  • If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your browsing.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law, including for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before personalised advertising cookies are used, through a consent message shown on the Website.

5. Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use the Website, for example which pages are visited, how visitors arrive, the type of device and browser, and approximate location (country or city). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information. It never receives the files you clean, because those files never leave your device. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies are off by default until you give consent. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

6. Other third-party services

  • Google Fonts: our pages load fonts from Google's servers, which receive your IP address in order to deliver them.
  • PDF component: when you clean a PDF, the Tool may load an open-source PDF library from a public content delivery network (unpkg.com) if it is not available from our own server. Only the library is downloaded. Your PDF is not sent anywhere.
  • External links: our guides link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and keep a record of the conversation. Please do not email us private files.

8. How we use information

We use the limited information described above to operate and secure the Website, show advertising that funds the free service, respond to your messages, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information for money.

9. Legal bases (EEA and UK visitors)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interests (running and securing the Website), your consent (personalised advertising and analytics cookies, where required), and legal obligations.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

California residents: under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out of personalised ads using the links in section 4. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To make a request, email admin@arecomservice.com. We may need to verify your request.

11. Data retention

We keep server logs and emails only as long as necessary for the purposes above, unless a longer period is required by law. Files you clean are never stored by us.

12. Security

Because files are processed locally in your browser, the main risk to your files is on your own device. The Website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

13. International transfers

We and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used.

14. Children

The Website is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
