Can You Remove Metadata from a Digitally Signed PDF?

GetFileClean TeamPDF · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

If a PDF has a digital signature, removing its metadata will make the signature invalid. Here's why, and what to do instead.

Why signatures break

A digital signature protects the exact bytes of the document at the moment of signing. Any change to the signed content, including metadata, means the document no longer matches what was signed. PDF readers then warn that the document has been modified.

The right order: clean first, then sign

  1. Finish the document.
  2. Remove metadata with GetFileClean.
  3. Then apply the digital signature.

The signed file will contain the signer's certificate information, which is expected and needed to verify the signature.

What if you've received a signed PDF?

Don't clean it if the signature needs to stay valid, for example contracts, official certificates or invoices that must be verified. Share it as it is.

E-signature platforms

Documents signed through e-signature services often include signer names, emails and audit information. This is part of their legal value. Removing it may make the document less useful as evidence.
