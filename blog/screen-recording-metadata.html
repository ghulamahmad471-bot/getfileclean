Do Screen Recordings Contain Metadata?

GetFileClean TeamVideo & Audio · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Screen recordings are used for tutorials, bug reports and showing someone how to do something. They usually carry less metadata than camera videos, but they're not empty.

Metadata in screen recordings

  • Creation dates
  • Software used to record, sometimes with version numbers
  • Device information on some phones
  • Title from the recording app

Location is less common because screen recordings don't use the camera, but don't assume it's never there.

The bigger risk is what's on screen

Screen recordings often capture far more than intended:

  • Notification pop-ups with messages and names
  • Browser tabs and bookmarks
  • Email addresses and account names
  • Open files and folder names
  • Passwords typed in plain text fields

Before you share

  1. Turn on Do Not Disturb before recording.
  2. Close unrelated tabs and apps.
  3. Watch the recording all the way through.
  4. Trim or blur anything sensitive.
