Healthcare and Metadata: Protecting Patient Information in Files

GetFileClean TeamProfessionals · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Healthcare organisations protect patient information carefully, but file metadata is easy to overlook.

Where metadata appears in healthcare work

  • Clinical photos taken on phones or cameras, with dates, devices and sometimes location.
  • Letters and reports written in Word, with author names, templates and document IDs.
  • Scanned documents, with scanner names and dates.
  • Recordings of consultations or dictation.

Why it matters

Metadata can connect a file to a patient, a clinic location or a time. Combined with other information, it can make data more identifiable.

Follow your organisation's systems first

Clinical images and records should usually be stored and shared through approved systems. Your organisation's policies and applicable laws come first. This article is general guidance only.

When cleaning helps

  • Sharing anonymised images for teaching, research or publication.
  • Sending patient information leaflets or templates outside the organisation.
  • Publishing documents online.

Why browser-based processing fits

GetFileClean doesn't upload files. All processing happens on your device, so files containing sensitive information aren't sent to an outside service just to be cleaned.

Remember

Removing metadata doesn't anonymise the content. Faces, tattoos, wristbands and visible text in photos still need handling.
