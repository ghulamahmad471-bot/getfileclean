Remove Metadata Offline: Does GetFileClean Work Without Internet?

GetFileClean TeamGetFileClean Guides · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Yes. Once GetFileClean has loaded, it can remove metadata offline. Here's how that works.

Why it works offline

GetFileClean doesn't send your files to a server. The cleaning code runs inside your browser, on your own computer or phone. After the page loads, cleaning photos, documents, videos and audio doesn't need the internet.

How to use it offline

  1. Open GetFileClean while connected.
  2. Disconnect from the internet if you want to.
  3. Add files, clean them and download the results as usual.

One exception: PDFs

The PDF cleaning component loads the first time you clean a PDF. To clean PDFs offline, clean one PDF while connected first, then it's ready to use for the rest of your session.

Why offline matters

  • Sensitive files: proof that nothing is being uploaded.
  • Restricted networks: some workplaces block uploads.
  • Travel: clean files on a plane or with poor signal.
  • Peace of mind: you can check that nothing is sent.
