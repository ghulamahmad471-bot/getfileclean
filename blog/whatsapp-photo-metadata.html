Does WhatsApp Remove Metadata from Photos? Photo vs Document

GetFileClean TeamSharing & Social Media · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

WhatsApp handles photo metadata differently depending on how you send a picture.

Sending as a normal photo

When you send an image the usual way, WhatsApp compresses it to save data. This process is widely reported to strip most EXIF metadata, including GPS. The downside is lower image quality.

Sending as a document

Many people send photos as a document to keep full quality. In that case, WhatsApp sends the original file unchanged, and that means all metadata goes with it: GPS location, device model, dates and more. The person receiving it can read everything.

HD photos

Newer versions offer an HD option for photos. Processing still happens, but behaviour can change between app versions, so don't rely on it for privacy.
