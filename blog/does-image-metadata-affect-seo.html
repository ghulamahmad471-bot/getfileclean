Does Image Metadata Affect SEO? EXIF, IPTC and Rankings

Ecommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Website owners sometimes worry that removing photo metadata will hurt their search rankings. Here's what we know about image metadata and SEO.

EXIF data and rankings

Google representatives have said publicly that Google doesn't rely on EXIF data such as camera model or GPS for ranking images in search. Removing EXIF is very unlikely to affect your rankings.

IPTC fields Google can display

Google Images can show certain IPTC photo metadata, such as creator, credit line and copyright notice, and licensing details. If you rely on those credits appearing, keep those fields, which means not stripping all metadata from those particular images.

What actually matters for image SEO

  • Descriptive file names, such as "red-leather-wallet.jpg"
  • Alt text describing the image
  • Relevant surrounding text on the page
  • Fast loading: compressed images in modern formats, sized correctly
  • Structured data for products where relevant
  • Image sitemaps for large sites

Metadata and page speed

Metadata adds bytes to every image. On its own it's usually small, but some photos carry large blocks of data or embedded thumbnails. Removing it trims file size, which supports faster pages.

The bottom line

For most sites, removing EXIF and other personal metadata has no downside for SEO and a small benefit for speed and privacy. Keep IPTC credit fields only on images where you want credits shown.
