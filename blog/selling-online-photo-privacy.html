Selling Online? Remove Photo Metadata Before You List Items

GetFileClean TeamEcommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Most people photograph items for sale at home: the sofa in the living room, the bike in the garage, the car in the driveway. If those photos carry GPS metadata, your listing could reveal exactly where valuable items are kept.

Where marketplace photos leak metadata

Large marketplaces often process uploaded images, but not every site does. Local classified sites, forums, small marketplaces and buyers who ask you to email extra photos may all receive the original files.

The risk

A listing for an expensive item plus a precise location is useful information for the wrong person. Even if the site strips metadata, photos you send directly to buyers by email or chat usually aren't cleaned.

How to list safely

  1. Take photos as usual.
  2. Run them through GetFileClean to remove GPS, device and date metadata.
  3. Upload the clean photos to your listing.
  4. Send buyers the same clean photos if they ask for more.

GetFileClean cleans dozens of photos at once and doesn't reduce quality, so your listing still looks sharp.

Other tips

  • Avoid showing your house number, street or car number plate in the background.
  • Meet buyers in public places where possible.
  • Don't share your full address until you need to.
