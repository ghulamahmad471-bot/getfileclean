Does Instagram Remove Metadata from Photos and Videos?

GetFileClean TeamSharing & Social Media · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Does Instagram remove metadata from the photos and videos you post? Mostly yes for public copies, but there's more to know.

Embedded metadata vs location tags

There are two different kinds of location on Instagram:

  • Embedded metadata: GPS coordinates inside the photo file from your camera. Instagram is widely reported to remove this from the images it serves to viewers.
  • Location tags: places you choose to add to a post. These are public by design and are not metadata. Instagram shows them to everyone who can see the post.

What Instagram may still receive

Like other platforms, Instagram receives your original file during upload, metadata included. What it stores and uses is covered by its privacy policy. If you don't want to hand over precise GPS data, clean files before posting.

Stories, Reels and DMs

Different features process media differently, and these systems change over time. Don't assume every feature treats files the same way.

Visual clues still matter

Metadata removal doesn't hide what's in the picture: house numbers, school uniforms, car number plates or a view from your window.
