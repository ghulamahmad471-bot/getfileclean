iPhone Videos Store Your Location: How to Remove It

Video & Audio · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Like photos, iPhone videos can save where they were recorded. The location is stored inside the video file, along with the device model and recording time.

What's stored in an iPhone video

  • GPS coordinates of the recording location
  • Make and model, for example the specific iPhone
  • Software version
  • Creation date and time

Turn off location for future videos

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.
  2. Tap Camera and choose Never.

This also stops location in new photos. Old videos still keep their location. See turn off location on the iPhone camera.

Share without location from Photos

In the share sheet, tap Options and turn off Location before sharing. This applies to that share only.
