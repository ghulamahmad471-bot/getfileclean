Dropshipping: Clean Supplier Photos Before Using Them

GetFileClean TeamEcommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Dropshippers often use product images supplied by manufacturers and wholesalers. Those images can carry the supplier's metadata.

What supplier images may contain

  • Software and editing history from the supplier's design team
  • Creator names, company names or copyright notices
  • Camera details and sometimes the location of the factory or studio
  • Comments and descriptions in other languages

Why clean them?

  • Supplier privacy: your sourcing is part of your business, and image files can point to who supplies you.
  • Consistency: images from many suppliers end up uniform and tidy.
  • Speed: removing extra data trims file sizes.

Respect image rights

Removing metadata doesn't change who owns an image. Only use supplier images you have permission to use, and don't remove copyright information from images in order to pass them off as your own. Where you have permission and the supplier doesn't require credit, cleaning is fine.

Workflow

  1. Download images from your supplier.
  2. Drop them into GetFileClean.
  3. Download all as a ZIP.
  4. Upload the clean images to your store.

Better still: your own photos

Your own photos help your listings stand out from other sellers using the same supplier images. Clean them too before uploading. See product photo metadata.
