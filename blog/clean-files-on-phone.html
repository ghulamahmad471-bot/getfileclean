How to Remove Metadata on Your Phone (iPhone and Android)

GetFileClean Guides · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

You don't need to install an app to remove metadata on your phone. GetFileClean works in mobile browsers.

On iPhone

  1. Open GetFileClean in Safari.
  2. Tap Choose files.
  3. Choose Photo Library for photos and videos, or Browse for files such as PDFs and documents.
  4. Wait for the report.
  5. Tap Download to save a clean copy. Safari may show a download prompt. Saved files usually go to the Files app, from where you can share them or save images to Photos.

On Android

  1. Open GetFileClean in Chrome.
  2. Tap Choose files and pick photos, videos or documents.
  3. Review the report.
  4. Tap Download. Files are saved to your Downloads folder.

Tips for phones

  • Download all as a ZIP if you clean many files, then share the ZIP.
  • Large videos depend on your phone's memory. Very long 4K videos are easier to clean on a computer.
  • HEIC photos are often converted to JPG automatically when picked through the browser on iPhone.

Why no app?

Apps need installing, updating and permission to access your photos. A browser tool only sees the files you choose, and nothing is uploaded.
