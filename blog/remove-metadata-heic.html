How to Remove Metadata from HEIC Photos

GetFileClean TeamPhoto Privacy · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format on modern iPhones. It stores the same kind of metadata as JPG, including GPS location, device model and dates. Here's how to remove metadata from HEIC files.

Why HEIC is trickier

HEIC is newer and less widely supported than JPG. Many tools can't edit HEIC directly, and some can't open it at all. Windows may need an extension to view HEIC files.

Option 1: Let your iPhone convert to JPG

If you share or pick photos through a browser or certain apps, iOS often converts HEIC to JPG automatically. You can also set your camera to save JPG: Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible. You'll still need to remove metadata from the JPG.

Option 2: Turn off location when sharing

Use the share sheet's Options menu and turn off Location. See remove metadata from iPhone photos.

Option 3: Convert and clean with GetFileClean

GetFileClean converts HEIC to a clean JPG in browsers that can open HEIC, such as Safari on Mac and iPhone. The new JPG contains no hidden metadata. If your browser can't open HEIC, the tool tells you, and you can convert to JPG first with your phone's settings above.

Will converting HEIC reduce quality?

Converting to JPG involves compression, but GetFileClean uses high quality settings, so the difference is very hard to see. If you need perfect originals, keep the HEIC for yourself and share the clean JPG.
