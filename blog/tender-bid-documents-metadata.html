Tenders and Bids: Check Metadata Before You Submit

Ecommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Proposals and tender responses are usually built from earlier ones. That's efficient, but metadata can reveal the reuse.

What bid documents can reveal

  • Title property still naming a previous client or project
  • Author and company fields showing a partner or subcontractor wrote sections
  • Creation dates years before the current tender
  • Template names
  • Comments and tracked changes with pricing discussions
  • Images taken on other projects, with their own locations and dates

Why it matters

Evaluators want to see a response written for their requirements. A properties field naming a competitor's client undermines that impression, even if the content is tailored.

A pre-submission checklist

  1. Accept or reject all tracked changes and delete comments.
  2. Search for previous client names in the visible text.
  3. Check headers, footers and hidden text.
  4. Export PDFs if the submission allows.
  5. Clean all files with GetFileClean.
  6. Open the final files and check their properties.
