How to Remove Metadata from Excel Files (XLSX)

GetFileClean TeamDocuments · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Spreadsheets travel widely: budgets, price lists, reports and exports. Here's how to remove metadata from Excel files before they leave your hands.

What metadata is in an XLSX file?

  • Author and Last Modified By
  • Company and Manager
  • Created and modified dates
  • Application and version
  • Custom properties from document management systems
  • Images embedded in the workbook with their own EXIF data

Method 1: Excel's Document Inspector

  1. Save a copy of the workbook.
  2. Go to File > Info > Check for Issues > Inspect Document.
  3. Click Inspect, then Remove All for Document Properties and Personal Information.
  4. Save.

The Inspector can also find hidden rows, columns and worksheets, comments and notes.

Hidden data that isn't metadata

Spreadsheets often hide information in plain sight: hidden sheets, hidden rows and columns, cells with white text, old pivot table caches and comments. These are part of the workbook's content. Read about hidden data in spreadsheets.

Sharing a spreadsheet with clients?

Consider exporting only the sheet they need to a new workbook, or exporting to PDF, then cleaning the result.
