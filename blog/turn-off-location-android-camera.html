How to Turn Off Location Tags on Your Android Camera

GetFileClean TeamPhoto Privacy · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Most Android camera apps can save location in your photos. Here's how to turn off location on your Android camera on the most common phones.

Samsung Galaxy

  1. Open the Camera app.
  2. Tap the settings gear.
  3. Turn off Location tags.

Google Pixel

  1. Open the Camera app.
  2. Open the settings menu.
  3. Turn off Save location.

Other Android phones

Look for a setting called location, geotag, GPS or save location in your camera app's settings. You can also remove the camera's location permission entirely:

  1. Open Settings > Apps.
  2. Find your camera app.
  3. Tap Permissions > Location and choose Don't allow.

Things to know

  • This only stops GPS for new photos. Old photos keep their location.
  • Dates, device model and camera settings are still saved.
  • Other apps that take photos, such as messaging apps, may have their own location permission.
