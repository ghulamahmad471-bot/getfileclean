Invoice and Quote PDFs: Metadata Businesses Forget to Check

Ecommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Invoices, quotes and receipts are sent to customers every day, usually as PDFs. Their metadata is rarely checked.

What business PDFs can reveal

  • Author: an employee's personal name or an accountant's name.
  • Creator and producer: your accounting or invoicing software.
  • Title: sometimes an internal file name such as a template for another client.
  • Dates: when the document was really created.

Is that a problem?

Often not. But it can be:

  • When you invoice on behalf of someone else.
  • When an outsourced bookkeeper prepares documents.
  • When template titles name other customers.
  • When you'd rather not reveal what software you use.

Don't remove what you need

Some PDFs, such as e-invoices in regulated formats or digitally signed invoices, must stay exactly as generated. Don't clean those. Check your local tax and invoicing rules. This is general information, not tax advice.
