Do Email Attachments Keep Metadata? (Yes, Here's What to Do)

Sharing & Social Media · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

When you attach a file to an email, it's sent exactly as it is. Email services don't strip attachment metadata, so everything inside the file reaches the recipient.

What the recipient can see

  • Photos: GPS location, device model, date and time.
  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint: author, last modified by, company, editing time and template.
  • PDFs: author, title, creating software and dates.
  • Videos: recording date, device and often location.

The email itself also contains technical headers, but those are separate from the file metadata.

Why this matters

Emails get forwarded, archived and stored for years. An attachment you send to one person can reach many others, metadata included. For job applications, client proposals and legal documents, leftover names and comments can be embarrassing or worse.
