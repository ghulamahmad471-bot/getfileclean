Voice Memos and Recordings: What Metadata Do They Contain?

GetFileClean TeamVideo & Audio · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Voice memos and recordings feel personal, and their files can carry personal metadata too.

What's in a voice recording file?

Many phone recording apps save M4A files, which use the same structure as MP4. They can contain:

  • Creation dates for the recording
  • Device and software information
  • Title set from the recording name, which may be a location name or your own label
  • Location, if the app was allowed to use it

When recordings get shared

  • Sending interview recordings to colleagues or transcription services
  • Sharing evidence recordings with lawyers or authorities
  • Posting audio clips online
  • Sending recordings in messaging apps as files

How to clean recordings

GetFileClean cleans M4A recordings by wiping metadata sections and clearing creation dates without re-encoding the audio. It also cleans MP3, WAV and FLAC files.

A word of caution about evidence

If a recording may be used as evidence, its original metadata, such as recording time, can matter. Keep the untouched original safely and share a cleaned copy only when appropriate. Ask a legal professional if you're unsure.
