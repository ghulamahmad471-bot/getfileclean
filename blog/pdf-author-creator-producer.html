PDF Author, Creator and Producer: What They Mean and How to Remove Them

GetFileClean TeamPDF · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Open a PDF's properties and you'll see three fields that often confuse people: Author, Creator and Producer.

Author

The person who wrote the document, usually filled in automatically from your user account. It may be your full name, a shortened account name, or someone else's name if you started from their file.

Creator

The application used to create the original document before it became a PDF, for example a word processor, spreadsheet, design app or browser. It shows what software you use and sometimes its version.

Producer

The software that actually generated the PDF file. This can be a PDF printer driver, a library used by a website, or an export feature. It often includes version numbers.

Why these fields matter

  • They can identify you by name.
  • They show which tools you use, which matters if you're presenting work as made a certain way.
  • Software versions can hint at outdated, vulnerable programs.
  • A "Producer" from an online converter shows you uploaded the document to that service.
