How to Remove Metadata on a Chromebook

Photo Privacy · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Windows and macOS have basic built-in tools for removing photo metadata. ChromeOS doesn't. Here's how to remove metadata on a Chromebook.

Why it's harder on ChromeOS

Many metadata tools are desktop programs that don't run on ChromeOS. Android apps may work on some Chromebooks but often have limited file access. Online upload tools work, but they send your files to someone else's server.

The easiest way: a browser-based tool

GetFileClean runs entirely in Chrome, which makes it a natural fit for Chromebooks:

  1. Open GetFileClean in Chrome.
  2. Click Choose files and pick files from your Downloads folder, Google Drive or a USB drive.
  3. Review the report.
  4. Download clean copies or a ZIP to your Downloads folder.

It works for photos, Word and Excel files, PDFs, videos and audio, and nothing is uploaded.

School and work Chromebooks

Managed Chromebooks may restrict downloads or some sites. If GetFileClean can't download files on a managed device, ask your administrator.

Google Drive files

Files stored in Google Drive keep their metadata. Download them, clean them with GetFileClean, then upload or share the clean copies.
