How to Remove Metadata from PowerPoint Presentations (PPTX)

Documents · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Presentations are often reused and passed between teams and companies. Here's how to remove metadata from PowerPoint files before you share a deck.

What metadata is in a PPTX file?

  • Author, Last Modified By, Company and Manager
  • Title and other descriptive properties, sometimes left over from an old deck
  • Created date, revision number and editing time
  • Template information
  • Photos inside slides with their own EXIF and GPS data

Method 1: PowerPoint's Document Inspector

  1. Save a copy.
  2. Go to File > Info > Check for Issues > Inspect Document.
  3. Inspect and remove Document Properties and Personal Information.
  4. Also check Comments, Off-slide content and Presentation notes.

Method 2: GetFileClean

Watch out for speaker notes

Speaker notes aren't metadata, but they're shared with the file. They may contain internal comments, pricing or remarks about the client. Delete them, or share a PDF export instead.

Off-slide content

Objects dragged off the edge of a slide are invisible during the presentation but remain in the file. The Document Inspector can find them.

Quick checklist for decks

  1. Remove speaker notes and comments.
  2. Check for off-slide objects.
  3. Clean properties and images with GetFileClean.
  4. Consider sending a PDF instead of the editable file.
