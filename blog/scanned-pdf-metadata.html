Do Scanned PDFs Have Metadata? Scanners, Phones and Copiers

PDF · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Scanned documents feel "clean" because they're just pictures of paper. But scanned PDFs usually have metadata too.

What scanned PDFs can contain

  • Producer and Creator: the scanner, copier or scanning app, often with model and version.
  • Dates: exact scan date and time.
  • Title: sometimes a default name from the device or app.
  • Author: occasionally a user name from the scanning device or the logged-in account.
  • OCR text layer: searchable text recognised from the image. Not metadata, but it means the words are stored as text in the file.

Phone scanning apps

Phone apps that scan documents may add their own app name and dates. Some may include location if the app has location permission.

Images inside the PDF

A scanned PDF is mostly images. Depending on how it was made, those images can carry their own embedded metadata. GetFileClean removes the PDF's document-level metadata and XMP. It doesn't change the scanned images themselves.

Check the content too

Scans can capture more than you meant: sticky notes, handwriting in margins, the next page bleeding through, or a document number. Look at every page before sending.
