Adobe Acrobat: Remove Hidden Information and Sanitize a PDF

PDF · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Adobe Acrobat Pro includes tools to remove hidden information from PDFs. Here's how they work and when you might use a free alternative.

Remove Hidden Information

In Acrobat Pro, the redaction tools include an option to remove hidden information. It scans the PDF and lists items such as:

  • Metadata
  • File attachments
  • Annotations and comments
  • Form fields
  • Hidden text and layers
  • Bookmarks
  • Embedded search indexes
  • Deleted or cropped content
  • Links and actions

You choose which items to remove and save the file. Menu names vary between Acrobat versions. Search Acrobat's tools for "hidden information" or "redact" if you can't find it.

Sanitize Document

Acrobat also offers a Sanitize option that removes all hidden information in one step, without asking about each item. It's quick but less selective.

When Acrobat is the right tool

  • You need to remove annotations, attachments or hidden layers as well as metadata.
  • You're doing true redaction of visible text.
  • You already have an Acrobat Pro licence.
