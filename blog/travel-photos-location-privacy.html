Travel Photos and Location Data: Share Without Revealing Where You Are

GetFileClean TeamPhoto Privacy · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Travel photos are fun to share, but they can reveal two things you may not want to broadcast: where you are right now, and that your home is empty.

What travel photo metadata contains

Each photo can store GPS coordinates and an exact time. A series of photos can map out your hotel, your daily movements and when you leave each place.

Keep location for yourself, not for others

Location data is great for your own memories: maps of your trip and photos grouped by city. The solution isn't to turn it off forever, but to remove it from the copies you share.

A simple travel routine

  1. Keep camera location on if you like having a map of your trip.
  2. Before sharing, run photos through GetFileClean to remove GPS and timestamps.
  3. Share the clean copies.
  4. Consider posting after you return home, not in real time.

Don't forget videos

Phone videos often contain location too. GetFileClean wipes location and creation dates from MP4 and MOV files without changing the video. Read why iPhone videos store location.

Visible clues

Hotel key cards, boarding passes and luggage tags in photos can reveal booking details. Crop or avoid them, even in clean files.
