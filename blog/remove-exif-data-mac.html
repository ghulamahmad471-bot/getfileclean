How to Remove EXIF Data and Location from Photos on Mac

GetFileClean TeamPhoto Privacy · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

macOS has a couple of built-in ways to remove location from photos, but neither removes everything. Here's how to remove EXIF data on a Mac.

Remove location with Preview

  1. Open the photo in Preview.
  2. Choose Tools > Show Inspector.
  3. Click the info tab, then the GPS tab if it appears.
  4. Click Remove Location Info.
  5. Save the file.

This removes GPS coordinates but leaves the device model, dates and other EXIF fields.

Hide location in the Photos app

  1. Select the photo in Photos.
  2. Choose Image > Location > Hide Location.

This hides the location within your library and when sharing from Photos, but it doesn't clean files you've already exported elsewhere.

Export without location from Photos

When exporting with File > Export, look for an option to include location information and turn it off. Other metadata may still be included.
