Telegram Photo Metadata: Compressed vs Uncompressed Files

GetFileClean TeamSharing & Social Media · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Telegram lets you send photos in two ways, and they handle metadata very differently.

Compressed photos

Sending an image as a regular photo compresses it. Compressed photos are generally reported to lose their EXIF metadata, including GPS. Quality is reduced.

Uncompressed files

Choosing to send without compression, or attaching an image as a file, sends the original. Everything inside the file, including location, camera model and dates, reaches the recipient. In public groups and channels, that can be a lot of people.

Why this matters on Telegram

Telegram is popular for large groups and channels where you may not know the members. Photographers and sellers often send uncompressed files to show full detail, without realising the metadata goes too.
