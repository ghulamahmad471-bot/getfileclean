eBay Listing Photos: Should You Remove Metadata?

Ecommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

eBay sellers upload large numbers of photos, often taken at home or in a storage space. Should you remove metadata from eBay photos?

What eBay listing photos can carry

  • GPS location of where items are stored
  • Timestamps showing when you list and work
  • Phone model and software

Doesn't eBay process uploaded photos?

Marketplaces generally resize and process images for display, which may remove metadata from the versions buyers see. But you shouldn't rely on that, and photos you send to buyers directly through messages, email or other channels may be the originals.

Reasons to clean anyway

  • Home sellers: keep your address private when selling valuable items.
  • Consistency: clean images behave the same on every channel you list on.
  • Smaller files: slightly faster uploads, especially in bulk.
  • Cross-listing: the same photos often go to other marketplaces, social media and your own store.

Other privacy tips

Avoid showing house numbers, mail with your address, or your car number plate in listing photos.
