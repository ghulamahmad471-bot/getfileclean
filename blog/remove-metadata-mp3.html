How to Remove Metadata from MP3 Files (ID3 Tags)

Video & Audio · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

MP3 files store their metadata in tags. Here's how to remove metadata from MP3 files without touching the audio.

What's in MP3 metadata?

MP3 files can contain up to three kinds of tags:

  • ID3v2 at the start of the file: title, artist, album, year, comments, cover art, lyrics, encoder, private data and custom tags.
  • ID3v1 in the last 128 bytes: a short, older tag format.
  • APE tags near the end, added by some software.

Why remove MP3 tags?

  • Recordings from your phone or recorder can include your name or device.
  • Cover art and comments can carry details you didn't mean to share.
  • Encoder information shows what software you used.
  • Private data frames from some apps can contain IDs.

Remove MP3 metadata without re-encoding

GetFileClean cuts the ID3v2, ID3v1 and APE tags out of the file. The audio frames aren't changed, so sound quality is identical.

Will my music player still work?

Yes. MP3s without tags play normally, but your music library won't show a title or artist. If you only want to remove some tags, use a tag editor instead.
