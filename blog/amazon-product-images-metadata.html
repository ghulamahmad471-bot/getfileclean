Amazon Product Images and Metadata: What Sellers Should Know

Ecommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Amazon sellers prepare main images, lifestyle photos and infographics for every listing. Here's what to know about metadata in product images.

What product image files carry

  • Camera data and GPS on photos you shoot yourself
  • Editing software history on infographics and edited photos
  • Supplier and designer details on images you received from others
  • Colour profiles, which are worth keeping

Follow Amazon's image requirements first

Amazon has its own rules for product images, covering backgrounds, dimensions, file types and content. Check Seller Central for the current requirements. Metadata cleaning doesn't change pixel dimensions or appearance, so it won't affect whether an image meets those rules.

Why clean product images

  • Supplier privacy: images from manufacturers can contain their company names, software or locations.
  • Designer files: freelance designers' details shouldn't travel to every channel.
  • Multi-channel selling: the same images go to your website, social media and ads.
  • Smaller files for faster uploads.
