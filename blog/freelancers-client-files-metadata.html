Freelancers: Clean Your Files Before Sending Them to Clients

Professionals · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Freelancers reuse templates, work for many clients and send lots of files. Metadata can reveal more about your business than you'd like.

What client files can reveal

  • Another client's name in a reused document's title or properties
  • Your working hours, from creation and modification dates
  • Software and versions you use, including trial or student versions
  • Total editing time in Office documents
  • Your location, from photos you took for the project
  • Subcontractors, if their names are in the author field

Professional delivery

Clean files look deliberate. A logo file or report full of another agency's metadata raises questions you don't need.

Keep your own records

Metadata can also be useful for you: version history, dates and authorship. Keep your working files and archive originals, then send clean copies.

A quick delivery routine

  1. Export final files.
  2. Drop them into GetFileClean and check the report.
  3. Download clean copies, or use Download all for one ZIP.
  4. Send the ZIP to your client.
