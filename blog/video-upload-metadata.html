Does Video Metadata Survive Uploading to Video Sites?

Video & Audio · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

When you upload a video to a large video platform, it's usually processed into new formats for streaming. Does the video metadata survive?

Viewers usually don't get your original file

Most large video platforms re-encode uploads for streaming. Viewers watch the platform's versions, not your original file, so the embedded metadata generally isn't passed on to them.

But the platform receives everything

The original upload, with location, device and dates, reaches the platform. Some platforms read information such as recording date or location from uploads to help organise videos. What happens next depends on the platform's settings and privacy policy.

Where originals do get shared

  • Sending video files directly by email, cloud links or messaging apps
  • Smaller sites and forums that host the original file
  • Client delivery of video projects
  • Platforms offering an "original file" download option
