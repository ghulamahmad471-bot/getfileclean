HR and Recruiting: Metadata in CVs, Offers and Policies

Professionals · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

HR teams create and receive many documents containing personal data. Metadata adds a hidden layer to that data.

Documents HR sends

  • Offer letters and contracts: template names, author names and earlier versions made for other candidates.
  • Policies and handbooks: internal comments and reviewer names.
  • Rejection letters: properties revealing a template or recruiter's name.

Documents HR receives

Candidates' CVs can contain metadata such as their current employer or other personal details. When forwarding CVs to hiring managers, consider whether all of that needs to travel. Anonymised recruitment processes should also remove metadata, not just names on the page.

Data protection

Metadata such as names can be personal data, and many privacy laws expect organisations to share only what's necessary. Cleaning files is a simple, practical step. See metadata and GDPR.

A simple HR routine

  1. Remove comments and tracked changes from outgoing documents.
  2. Clean the files with GetFileClean before sending.
  3. For anonymised hiring, clean candidate files before forwarding.
