Real Estate Photos and Metadata: What Agents Should Know

Professionals · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Property listings are mostly public, so why should agents care about photo metadata? There are a few good reasons.

Situations where it matters

  • Rental and private listings: the exact address may be withheld until viewing, but GPS data in photos can reveal it.
  • Tenants' privacy: photos taken in occupied homes can carry dates showing when the occupants are away.
  • Agents' own devices: device models and dates are attached to every photo.
  • Photos taken elsewhere: shots of a neighbourhood or area photographed from your own home or office.

Large listing sites vs direct sharing

Big property portals may process images. But photos are also emailed to sellers, sent to buyers, shared in cloud folders and posted on agency websites, where originals are often kept.

Smaller files, faster pages

Removing metadata also makes image files slightly smaller, which helps listing pages load faster.
