Product Photo Metadata: A Guide for Online Store Owners

Ecommerce & Business · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Online sellers take and upload hundreds of product photos. Each one may carry metadata that has nothing to do with the product.

What product photos can reveal

  • Where your stock is kept: GPS coordinates of your home, warehouse or studio.
  • When you work: timestamps on every photo.
  • Your devices and software.
  • Supplier details: images received from suppliers may carry their names, locations or software.

Why it matters for your business

  • Security: stock locations are useful information for thieves.
  • Competitive privacy: competitors can learn about your setup or suppliers.
  • Page speed: metadata adds a little to every image's file size. Across a large catalogue, cleaner files mean slightly faster pages.
  • Professionalism: clean images look and behave consistently.

Marketplace and store platforms

Some marketplaces process images, but you also send photos to customers, wholesale buyers, ad platforms and designers. Cleaning at the source covers every channel.
