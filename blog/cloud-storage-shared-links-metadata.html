Shared Cloud Links Keep Your File Metadata (Drive, Dropbox, iCloud)

Sharing & Social Media · 2 min read · Updated 2026-11-25

Cloud storage is the easiest way to share large files, but it usually shares the original file, with all its metadata.

What happens when you share a link

When someone opens a shared link and downloads a file, they generally get exactly what you uploaded. For photos, that can include GPS location and device details. For documents and PDFs, it includes author names, company fields and editing history.

Photo services are different from file storage

Photo services such as Google Photos and iCloud Photos have their own sharing settings, including options to leave out location in some cases. Plain file storage such as Drive folders and Dropbox doesn't usually change your files.

The "set and forget" problem

Shared folders often stay open for months. Files added later inherit the same sharing, and their metadata goes along with them.

A safer workflow

  1. Create a separate "to share" folder.
  2. Clean files with GetFileClean before putting them in that folder.
  3. Share only that folder.
