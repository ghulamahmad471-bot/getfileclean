GetFileClean, also written **Get File Clean**, is a free online tool that removes hidden metadata from your files: GPS location in photos, author names in documents, device details in videos and much more. It also publishes plain-English guides to help people understand what their files reveal.

## Why we built GetFileClean

Most people have no idea that a photo can contain the exact location where it was taken, or that a Word document can carry the name of the person who wrote it and the company they work for. Most metadata removal tools also ask you to upload your files to their servers, which means handing private files to a stranger in order to protect your privacy.

We built GetFileClean to do the opposite. The cleaning happens inside your own web browser. **Your files are never uploaded to us or anyone else.**

## What GetFileClean does

- Removes EXIF, GPS, XMP and IPTC metadata from JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images without reducing quality.
- Removes author, company, dates and other properties from Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OpenDocument files.
- Removes document information and XMP metadata from PDF files.
- Removes location, device and date metadata from MP4 and MOV videos without re-encoding.
- Removes tags from MP3, M4A, FLAC and WAV audio files.
- Cleans supported files inside ZIP archives.

There are no file size limits, no file count limits and no sign-up.
